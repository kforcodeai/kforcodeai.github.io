## What is context compaction in an agent harness?

Context compaction is how a harness decides what to stop sending the model. Three things
are true and most implementations get all three wrong:

- **Deletion is cheaper than summarization** — summarizing costs an extra model call to
  produce something lossier than the original.
- **Tool results are the bulk of the transcript** — and the agent already extracted what
  it needed from them.
- **Compaction is a ladder, not a switch** — recoverable reductions first, paid summary last.

**Thesis:** evict stale tool results before you summarize conversation, and keep every
evicted result recoverable by reference.

## Why this way and not the other?

| Option | What you gain | What you pay | Choose it when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Truncate oldest | Trivial to implement, zero extra calls | Silently drops the task definition | Never, in a long-running agent |
| Evict tool results | Near-lossless, no model call | Needs artifact storage + reference discipline | Default |
| Summarize conversation | Preserves narrative continuity | An extra call, and lossy in ways you cannot audit | After eviction is exhausted |
| Learned compression | Highest ratio | Genuinely lossy; hard to debug | Only at a hard context limit |
